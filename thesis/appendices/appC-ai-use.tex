\chapter{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools}\label{app:ai}

In accordance with the Texas Tech University Graduate School policy on the use of artificial-intelligence and large-language-model tools in theses and dissertations, the candidate declares that generative-AI tools were used in the preparation of this document in the following capacities and in no others.

The tool used was Claude (Anthropic), a large-language-model assistant. It was used for three purposes: language editing, that is, suggesting revisions to the candidate's own draft prose for grammar, clarity and concision; \LaTeX{} formatting assistance, that is, help with the typesetting of tables, figures drawn in TikZ and pgfplots, bibliography entries and cross-references so that the document conforms to the Graduate School formatting manual; and literature-organization support, that is, help in collating, deduplicating and formatting bibliographic records of the candidate's own publications and of the related work that the candidate had already identified. The tool was not used to design experiments, to generate or analyse data, to derive results or to draw scientific conclusions. All research described in this dissertation, including the methods, software, experiments, analyses and interpretations, was conceived and carried out by the candidate and the coauthors named in the authorship statements at the head of each chapter, and every statement of fact, every number and every citation in the text was verified by the candidate against the original sources. The candidate accepts full responsibility for the content of this document. No AI tool is an author of this dissertation or of any of the publications on which it is based.
